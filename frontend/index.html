Architecture & Data Flow

How the SmartCity AI Platform Operates

An end-to-end telemetry architecture connecting field sensors, AI reasoning models, and municipal service teams directly with citizens.

1

IoT Telemetry & Sensors

Over 100+ IoT nodes, SCADA flow sensors, RFID waste tags, adaptive CCTV optical feeds, and parking detectors stream live readings every second.

2

ICCC & Grounded AI

Machine learning models analyze patterns, predict traffic congestion bottlenecks, monitor hospital bed availability, and detect anomalous pressure drops.

3

Automated Dispatch

Emergency ambulances receive dynamic green light preemption corridors, garbage trucks receive optimized collection routes, and police units coordinate.

4

Transparent Citizen Delivery

Citizens access real-time hospital queues, reserve parking bays, verify e-Challans, and track grievances with binding municipal SLAs.

Mission & Governance

About Gorakhpur Smart City

Gorakhpur Smart City Mission is an urban transformation initiative by the Government of Uttar Pradesh and the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs.

By combining sustainable physical infrastructure with state-of-the-art information technology, the platform enhances quality of life, delivers accountable municipal services, and empowers citizens through digital inclusion.

🌱
Clean & Sustainable Development
Solar street lighting, eco-friendly transit corridors, and Ramgarh Tal wetland conservation.
🏥
World-Class Healthcare Integration
Direct real-time digital linking with AIIMS Gorakhpur and BRD Medical College.
🛡️
Accountable Civic SLAs
Every complaint is tracked on a public ledger with guaranteed resolution timelines.
