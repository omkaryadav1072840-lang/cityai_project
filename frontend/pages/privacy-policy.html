Official Municipal Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 29, 2026 | Last Updated: Version 2.4

1. Introduction & Scope

SmartCity AI ("Platform", "we", "our") is the integrated civic technology management platform operating in Gorakhpur and affiliated smart municipal regions. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and digital rights of all citizens, staff personnel, doctors, and civic administrators.

This Privacy Policy details what information is collected, how it is processed and protected, and your rights concerning your personal data in accordance with applicable data protection principles.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimal information necessary to deliver municipal and urban services:

  • Account & Identity Data: Name, mobile phone number, email address, password hash (salted and encrypted; never stored in plaintext), residential ward, and role classification (Citizen, Staff, Doctor, Admin).
  • Location Telemetry: Precise or approximate GPS coordinates when explicitly permitted by you to locate nearest emergency services, live ambulances, municipal hospitals, parking slots, and traffic congestion points.
  • Civic & Service Booking Records: Parking slot reservations, vehicle numbers, waste pickup dispatch requests, civic complaint tickets, and municipal utility status inquiries.
  • Hospital & Healthcare Information: Doctor appointment scheduling, OPD queue numbers, bed occupancy inquiries, and authenticated access to prescription documents or diagnostic test reports.
  • AI Chatbot Interactions: Queries submitted to the SmartCity Grounded Assistant are processed to retrieve database answers (e.g. bed availability, route conditions). Chat history is not sold or shared with commercial advertising brokers.

3. Hospital & Health-Related Information Handling

Notice Regarding Clinical Privacy: Medical records, prescriptions, and diagnostic lab reports stored within the hospital subsystem are protected by Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and restricted strictly to authenticated patients, assigned doctors, and authorized hospital desk staff.

Public hospital dashboards only aggregate anonymous bed availability counts and doctor on-duty status. They never expose patient names, diagnoses, or personal health records publicly.

4. Cookies & Privacy-Preserving Analytics

We use strictly necessary session cookies and token-based storage (JWT in localStorage/sessionStorage) to maintain your login state securely.

Our analytics pipeline operates on privacy-by-design principles: we track aggregated counts of system actions (e.g., total hospital searches, parking inquiries, traffic queries) without logging personal identification numbers, clinical details, or contact information. You may accept or decline non-essential cookies via our Cookie Consent banner.

5. Data Security & Storage

SmartCity AI employs enterprise-grade cybersecurity controls:

  • HTTPS transport encryption (TLS) for all client-server communications in production.
  • Argon2/Bcrypt salted hashing for all user and staff credentials.
  • Strict JWT verification and token expiration mechanisms.
  • Parameterized database queries preventing SQL injection vulnerabilities.
  • Input sanitization protecting against Cross-Site Scripting (XSS).
  • DDoS mitigation and IP-based rate limiting on sensitive authentication and SOS endpoints.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain account records for as long as your civic profile remains active. Temporary booking and transit records (such as completed parking reservations or resolved waste requests) are maintained for audit trails and statistical planning for a maximum period of 36 months, after which they are archived or pseudonymized.

7. User Rights

As a registered citizen or user of SmartCity AI, you have the right to:

  • Review your profile data via the Citizen Profile modal.
  • Correct inaccurate contact or residential ward information.
  • Request an export of your civic activity history.
  • Revoke browser location permissions at any time via your device settings.
