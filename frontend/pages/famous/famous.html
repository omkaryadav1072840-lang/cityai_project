About Ramgarh Taal

Ramgarh Taal is an expansive 1,800-acre natural lake in Gorakhpur, transformed into a world-class waterfront tourist hub featuring pedestrian walkways, decorative lighting, paddle and speed boating, laser light shows, and floating restaurants.
