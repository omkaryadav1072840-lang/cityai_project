Voluntary Blood Donation Guidelines

  • Age Eligibility: Donors must be aged between 18 and 65 years.
  • Weight & Hemoglobin: Minimum weight of 45 kg and Hb level ≥ 12.5 g/dL.
  • Donation Intervals: Minimum 90 days between whole blood donations for males, 120 days for females.
  • Privacy Guarantee: Your contact telephone number is stored confidentially and is never made publicly searchable. Authorized hospital phlebotomy staff will contact you only when an exact emergency match arises.
  • Consent Revocation: You retain the full legal right to pause availability or withdraw donor consent at any time without penalty.
